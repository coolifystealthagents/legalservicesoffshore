## 1. Litigation hold acknowledgement register

- Slug: `litigation-hold-acknowledgement-register-offshore-support`
- Pillar: litigation operations
- Decision served: whether a firm can delegate acknowledgement follow-up without delegating hold scope, recipient selection, or compliance decisions.
- Structure: define the acknowledgement population; distinguish delivery, receipt, acknowledgement, and exception; map reminder authority; handle departed custodians and shared mailboxes; design counsel review; close with a seven-day pilot.
- Original example: an approved recipient roster contains an employee whose mailbox auto-replies that the person has left. The support worker records the delivery evidence and stops; counsel decides whether the recipient population changes.
- Reader outcome: a field-level register and escalation design that preserves counsel control.
- Sources: ABA Formal Opinion 08-451; ABA Model Rule 5.3; NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0.
